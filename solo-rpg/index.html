⚔ Wanderer's Oracle

A solo dungeon-crawl RPG. Roll the dice, ask the Oracle, write your own story.

How to Play

1. Build your Wanderer. Spend 8 points across four stats (min 1, max 5 each):

  • Might — combat power. Added to your attack rolls and damage.
  • Grit — toughness. Sets your max HP and defense.
  • Wits — cunning. Used for traps, puzzles, and fleeing danger.
  • Luck — fortune. Each point gives you a reroll token, spendable once per roll.

2. Explore. Click Venture Forward to advance one room deeper into the dungeon. Every room brings a random encounter: monsters, traps, treasure, a shrine to rest at, or an empty hall. Every 5th room hides a Guardian; room 20 holds the Dungeon's final boss.

3. Resolve encounters with dice. Combat and traps are resolved with a d20 + the relevant stat against a difficulty number. You can spend a Luck token to reroll any single roll.

4. Consult the Oracle. At any point, type a yes/no question about your story ("Is the door trapped?", "Does the old man trust me?"), pick how likely you think the answer is, and let the Oracle decide. Use it to improvise your own narrative between encounters — that's the "solo" part of solo RPG.

5. Survive to room 20 and defeat the final boss to win. If your HP hits 0, your run ends — but your journal remains, so you can read the story you told.

Your game auto-saves in this browser. Use Download Journal anytime to save your story as a text file.
