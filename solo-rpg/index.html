Wanderer's Oracle

A solo dungeon crawl. Twenty rooms, one torch, and dice that tell the story.

How to play

Build your wanderer. Every stat starts at 1. Spend 8 points; no stat can start above 5.

  • Might is added to your attack rolls. Your damage is d6 + 1 + half your Might (rounded up).
  • Grit sets your max HP (10 + Grit × 3).
  • Wits sets your Defense (10 + Wits) and helps you dodge traps and flee from ordinary monsters.
  • Luck is how many Luck tokens you start with.

Go deeper. Each tap of Venture moves you one room forward. A room can hold a monster, a trap, treasure, a shrine or nothing at all. Rooms 5, 10 and 15 hold Guardians. Room 20 holds the Hollow Sovereign. Beat it to win.

Fight with dice. An attack is d20 + Might against the enemy's Defense. A natural 20 always hits and deals an extra d6; a natural 1 always misses. After your attack, the enemy swings back at your Defense.

Spend Luck on a Lucky Strike. Roll two d20s and keep the higher. If it hits, it deals an extra d6. Shrines refill one spent token.

Grow stronger. Each Guardian you defeat grants a Boon: +1 Might, +1 Grit, +1 Wits, +1 Luck token and a full heal.

Manage supplies. Resting eats one Ration and restores some HP. Healing Herbs can be used any time, even mid-fight, but the enemy gets a swing while you use one. You can flee from ordinary monsters, but never from Guardians or the Sovereign.

Ask the Oracle. Solo play means you are the storyteller. Ask a yes-or-no question about the world ("Is someone following me?"), pick how likely it seems, and the d20 answers. Roll a story prompt when you need an idea. Keys, rings and maps you find are story items: use them with the Oracle to shape what happens.

Your run saves automatically in this browser. Use Copy journal to keep your story somewhere else.
